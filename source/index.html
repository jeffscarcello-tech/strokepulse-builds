iPhone Setup

1. Add to Home Screen. In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open StrokePulse from that icon.


2. Allow Location and Motion. The first time you press START, iPhone asks for both. Location gives speed, split and distance; Motion gives stroke rate.


3. Keep the screen on. The app asks the iPhone to stay awake while recording. To be safe, set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never while on the water. If the screen locks, GPS and stroke rate pause.


4. Mount it solidly. Use a waterproof case on a rigid mount (rigger or footboard), pointing the same way every time. A loose phone gives a messy stroke rate.


5. Wait for green GPS. Give it 20–60 seconds outside before starting. Green = under 10 m accuracy.


6. Battery. Screen-on GPS uses roughly 15–25% per hour. Tap ☀︎ on the main screen for Bright mode in direct sun.


Stopping: tap STOP, then tap END & SAVE. If you don't confirm within 5 seconds, it keeps recording, so a splash can't end your session.


Fields: tap any number on the live screen to choose what it shows.
